# Reincarnation & the Soul Tree

**Research snapshot: 13 November 2026.** This is a practical farming-oriented
plan, not a mathematically proven optimum or live character data. Costs and
effects come from the [official public Soul Tree
data](https://api.immortalzenith.com/api/v1/public/wiki/soultree); check the
game's current node panel and reward preview before committing.

## The short answer

Reincarnation becomes available at **Nascent Soul**, but that is an unlock,
not an instruction to reset immediately. Continue climbing while the next
breakthrough is reasonably attainable. Reincarnate when further progress
slows and the displayed Dao Soul Point (DSP) reward will buy an upgrade you
actually want.

For someone who repeatedly farms beasts, a useful first target is **100 DSP**:
Tireless Fist plus one rank each of Flowing Meridians and Enlightened Heart.
This is a recommendation, not a guaranteed reward at a specific realm. If
reaching it takes disproportionately long, choose a smaller package instead.

## What survives and what resets

Rebirth normally resets your realm to Human, along with your inventory,
techniques, crafting specializations, sect ties and other life-specific
progress. Your cultivation age resets and your bloodline and physique are
rerolled unless their retention options are unlocked.

Your soul keeps its **Soul Tree, unspent DSP, reincarnation count and highest
realm ever reached**. Additional possessions or progress carry over only when
an appropriate node allows it and the rebirth flow permits the selection.
The game's warning also says items exclusive to the Celestial Caravan are kept.
Read the current confirmation screens for the complete rules.

The preview shows the DSP reward before confirmation. The in-game guide says
the award rises steeply with the realm reached during the life, with a
contribution from final Dao Power. The full server formula is not known here:
do not assume a fixed DSP award for a given realm.

Each previous reincarnation grants **+0.25× catch-up Qi generation** while
climbing back. It switches off on reaching your previous highest realm, so it
is not a permanent multiplier beyond your peak.

## When to reincarnate

**Keep going** if another major realm looks attainable without a long detour,
or if you are still earning important upgrades and enjoying this life.
**Consider rebirth** when progress has slowed and the preview will fund a
useful purchase. Balance extra points against the time spent reaching them:
the highest single payout is not automatically the best rate of progress.

There is no verified universal best first-reincarnation realm. A valuable
current trait, manual or item also changes the decision: if you cannot yet
retain it, that loss is part of the cost.

## First-rebirth budgets

### Farming-oriented package: 100 DSP

| Priority | Node                      | Cost | Effect                                                  |
| -------- | ------------------------- | ---: | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1        | Tireless Fist             |   70 | Unlocks Auto-Combat for a foe after 10 wins against it. |
| 2        | Flowing Meridians, rank 1 |   15 | +10% Qi generation.                                     |
| 3        | Enlightened Heart, rank 1 |   15 | +10% technique comprehension rate.                      |

Tireless Fist is especially relevant if you spend a lot of time repeating beast
fights. Auto-Combat is an **action**, including offline; it is not a free
additional activity. Exploring and Auto-Combat also gather a quarter of your
cultivation Qi and comprehension. Compare its results against manual hunts:
Bonus Yield's manual-hunt rewards should not be assumed to apply to auto fights.

If you primarily cultivate rather than farm, buy the two 15-point cultivation
roots first and consider their additional ranks before automation. Their
second ranks cost 21 each. At 70–99 DSP, buy Tireless Fist first only if it
fits your playstyle; with 30–69 DSP, the two roots are a reasonable start.

### Add a retained manual: 217 DSP total

Starting with the 100-DSP package above:

1. **Echo of the Soul**: 38 DSP after Enlightened Heart rank 1. Carry your
   active Soul technique, but at **level 0**.
2. **Remnant Soul Memory**: 79 DSP after Echo. The retained technique returns
   at up to **level 10**, if it reached that level.

The corresponding Mind and Body paths have the same initial costs. Pick the
aspect whose active manual is actually worth keeping. Check trait requirements:
a manual may be unavailable to a newly rolled bloodline or physique.

## Cultivation: build the foundation

Costs below are **cumulative from zero** and include the listed prerequisites.
Subtract any ranks already purchased; do not pay for a shared prerequisite
twice.

| Goal                        | Required route                          |     Total |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------: |
| +10% Qi generation          | Flowing Meridians 1                     |    15 DSP |
| Begin at Body Tempering     | Flowing Meridians 1 → Rooted Foundation |    90 DSP |
| Begin at Qi Condensation    | Above → Tempered Rebirth                |   338 DSP |
| Begin at Core Formation     | Above → Soul-Forged Start               | 1,013 DSP |
| Choose old or new bloodline | Above → Bloodline Memory                | 2,329 DSP |
| Choose old or new physique  | Above → Physique Memory                 | 2,329 DSP |

Both trait-memory nodes share the starting-realm path: **3,645 DSP from zero**
buys both. These are long-term goals, not realistic assumptions about the
first reward.

Buy realm skips when replaying those realms has become a significant portion
of each run. Catch-up Qi may already make the earliest levels quick, so compare
the time saved with other upgrades. Trait memory becomes more compelling when
your existing trait is rare or central to your build.

- **Many Roads:** 38 DSP after Enlightened Heart 1 for one extra origin choice
  per rank. More choice does not guarantee a better origin.
- **Long Years:** 68 DSP after Enlightened Heart 1 for 40 additional starting
  lifespan years per rank. Prioritize it if lifespan is the limiting factor.
- **Unbroken Meridians:** retains a trace of combat attributes: **0.05% per
  rank**, not 5%. Its eventual impact depends on the life being carried over.

## Combat: automate, hunt and preserve

Tireless Fist costs 70 DSP and has no node prerequisite. For manual Bonus Yield
hunting, the next route is:

| Path from zero                                  |   Total |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------: |
| Tireless Fist → Beast Lore 1 → Bountiful Hunt 1 | 355 DSP |
| Above → Abundant Harvest 1                      | 760 DSP |

Beast Lore adds 4% damage per rank against beasts defeated in a past life.
Bountiful Hunt adds 10 daily Bonus Yield charges per rank to the base 30;
Abundant Harvest improves Bonus Yield rewards. These nodes are most attractive
when you regularly use the **manual** hunts.

For possessions, **Hoarder's Soul** costs 25 DSP for one carryable item slot.
**Worthy Vessels**, 45 DSP after it, increases eligible rarity. **Masterwork
Bond**, 315 DSP after Worthy Vessels, allows masterworked carryable items.
Inspect the rebirth selection screen for item restrictions; a slot does not
keep an entire inventory.

**Fans Instinct** exists for fan mastery, but it is deep in a prerequisite
chain. Its listed cost alone is not the price of reaching it. Add all missing
prerequisites before comparing it with broader upgrades.

## Exploration: work toward a second action

**Soul Wandering** is a useful long-term idle-play milestone:

1. Buy **Swift Wanderer**, the search-interval root, to rank 5: **135 DSP**
   in total (15 + 21 + 27 + 33 + 39).
2. Buy **Soul Wandering**: **420 DSP**.
3. Total from zero: **555 DSP**.

It enables a second action at 50% efficiency. Check the available action
combinations in-game. **Twin Paths** is a separate 450-DSP follow-up that
permits two exploration zones.

There are two nodes named Swift Wanderer in the public data: one reduces
**search interval**, the other reduces **travel time**. The search-interval
root is the prerequisite for Soul Wandering. Region retention and raw-material
retention are useful if repeatedly reopening zones or replacing materials is
the main time sink.

## Crafting: preserve what you use

| Node            |                    Initial cost | When it matters                                       |
| --------------- | ------------------------------: | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Forge Memory    |                          20 DSP | Keep an unlocked crafting slot through rebirth.       |
| Practiced Hands |                          25 DSP | Begin one mastery rank higher, if previously reached. |
| Deft Artisan    |     23 DSP after Forge Memory 1 | Reduce craft time by 5% per rank.                     |
| Polymath Soul   | 240 DSP after Practiced Hands 1 | Unlock another crafting specialization.               |

These are strong quality-of-life choices for an active crafter. For a character
focused on farming and cultivation, buy them when preserving crafting saves
more time than the alternative.

## A plan across lives

These are **priority stages**, not claims about exactly how many points a
particular reincarnation awards.

### First rebirth

Inspect the reward preview at Nascent Soul and at later milestones. Push
further if the next major realm is achievable at reasonable cost. If the
budget and your goals fit, buy the 100-DSP farming package. On the next life,
defeat your desired beast ten times and test its Auto-Combat results.

### Second life

Use the catch-up bonus to return toward your former peak and, where practical,
push into a higher realm before resetting again. Add a manual-memory path if
rebuilding comprehension hurts, or starting-realm nodes if replaying early
realms takes too much time. If you use your daily manual hunt charges, consider
the route toward Bountiful Hunt.

### Later lives

Pick the path that addresses your actual bottleneck:

- **Repeated early realms:** starting-realm nodes.
- **Lost comprehension:** deeper Memory nodes in your important aspect.
- **Idle multitasking:** the 555-DSP route to Soul Wandering.
- **Manual beast farming:** Bountiful Hunt, then Abundant Harvest.
- **Rare trait loss:** the expensive Bloodline or Physique Memory route.
- **Crafting rebuild:** Forge Memory and mastery-related nodes.

Do not spread points across all four trees merely for variety. A completed
route can be more useful than isolated ranks, while cheap roots can still solve
an immediate problem.

## Before confirming rebirth

1. Read the **reward preview** and total the prerequisites for your plan.
2. Read the loss warning and check the actual carryover selection.
3. Set the manual you intend to retain as active in its aspect.
4. Verify whether your bloodline and physique can be retained; both require
   their own memory nodes.
5. Account for possessions, crafting progress and sect ties.
6. Choose your new origin based on its lifetime bonuses **and drawbacks**, not
   rarity alone.

The rebirth wizard banks points, allows Soul Tree spending, and proceeds to
carryover and the new character choices. **Treat confirmation as a commitment**,
not as a way to collect points and keep playing the old life.
