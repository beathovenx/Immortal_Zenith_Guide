# Fast progression: cultivation and Soul Wandering

**Research snapshot: 14 November 2026.** This is a practical
cultivation-first plan for someone who also wants Soul Wandering. It is not a
proven global speedrun optimum. Soul Tree prices and effects use the local
1.31-era public wiki snapshot; check the current game before spending.

## The route at a glance

1. Keep useful cultivation running and spend active time on the Mortal Road,
   breakthroughs, necessary gear and high-value Bonus Yield hunts.
2. Reach Nascent Soul, then compare the rebirth reward with the time needed
   for the next milestone. Do not reset immediately just because it unlocks.
3. Invest early in **Flowing Meridians** for Qi and **Enlightened Heart** for
   comprehension. Make **Soul Wandering** the next major quality-of-life goal.
4. After the second action, strengthen your Qi engine and buy retention,
   starting-realm skips or hunting perks when they address your real bottleneck.

The order below deliberately values a second action; if you only measure
standstill cultivation Qi, buying more Qi ranks first may be better. No
simulator or live character data proves one universal order.

## A concrete Soul Tree spending plan

All budgets are **cumulative from an empty tree**, not the price of each
individual step. Rank costs grow; subtract purchases you already own and
include prerequisites only once. You can save unspent DSP across lives.

|  Budget | Cumulative purchases                                                        | Why                                                                   |
| ------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  30 DSP | Flowing Meridians 1 + Enlightened Heart 1                                   | +10% Qi generation and +10% technique comprehension.                  |
|  78 DSP | Flowing Meridians 3 + Enlightened Heart 1                                   | Three Qi ranks and one comprehension rank; a low-cost foundation.     |
| 633 DSP | The 78-DSP foundation + Swift Wanderer (search interval) 5 + Soul Wandering | Unlock the second action; the exploration route itself costs 555 DSP. |
| 775 DSP | Above + Flowing Meridians 5 + Tireless Fist                                 | More Qi and auto-combat after its foe-specific unlock.                |

These are **purchase milestones**, not predictions of how much a first,
second or later rebirth pays. If you have 555 DSP but not 633, you can
choose Soul Wandering first instead of the 78-DSP foundation; weigh an
immediate second action against the delay to permanent Qi ranks.

### Exact Soul Wandering path

In the **Exploration** tree, buy the **search-interval** Swift Wanderer to
rank 5: **15 + 21 + 27 + 33 + 39 = 135 DSP**. Then buy **Soul Wandering**
for **420 DSP**. Total: **555 DSP from zero**.

There is a different node also named Swift Wanderer that reduces **travel
time**; it is not the prerequisite. The five search ranks reduce search
interval by 25% according to their description; they are useful while
exploring, but are not a direct cultivation-rate bonus.

## Use Soul Wandering correctly

The game's guide describes a **second action at 50% efficiency**. After
unlocking the node, use **Second Path** on the character sheet and select an
available action.

- If your main action is cultivation, the client disables a second
  cultivation action: don't assume two cultivation slots stack.
- If another primary action is running, Second Path offers cultivation at
  half rate. Check the actual activity and rate shown on your sheet.
- Second Path can offer exploration or auto-combat; auto-combat requires
  **Tireless Fist** and at least **10 wins against the chosen foe**.
- **Twin Paths** costs another **450 DSP** after Soul Wandering and enables
  two exploration zones at once. It is optional, not part of the 555-DSP
  unlock and not the first priority for pure cultivation.

Patch 1.31 also says exploration and auto-combat gather a quarter of
cultivation Qi and comprehension while active. The exact combined rate and
interaction between those passive gains and a second action are not verified
here: **do not simply add 25% and 50%**. Compare the in-game rate for your
chosen action pairing. Soul Wandering is primarily a way to do more at once,
not evidence of a guaranteed increase to pure cultivation speed.

## Increase cultivation speed

### Permanent Qi and comprehension

**Flowing Meridians** gives +10% Qi generation per rank, up to 10 ranks.
Rank costs are 15, 21, 27, 33, 39, 45, 51, 57, 63 and 69 DSP: **135 DSP
for rank 5** and **420 DSP for rank 10**. Raise it when Qi accumulation is
the bottleneck. **Eternal Spring** follows rank 10: its first rank costs
90 DSP for +1% Qi generation, so reaching it from zero costs **510 DSP**.
Its value versus other purchases depends on your actual rate and goals.

**Enlightened Heart** adds +10% technique comprehension per rank, with the
same first ten rank costs. It does not directly state a Qi-generation bonus:
buy more ranks when learning manuals is slowing your build.

For illustration only, if all else stays equal and the displayed Qi rate
really rises from 100 to 110 Qi/s, a 10,000-Qi pool fills in about 91 seconds
instead of 100. Real stacking, caps, switching actions, offline behavior and
breakthrough requirements may change the result. Compare displayed rates,
not just the sum of perk descriptions.

### Manuals, origin and Qi Cap

Use your best **available** manual for your goal, checking both its Qi
generation and useful comprehension milestones. Switching manuals no longer
costs accumulated Qi under patch 1.30. An origin with strong Qi or
comprehension bonuses can help throughout a life, but origin offers are
random; compare drawbacks and don't assume a specific roll.

Qi Cap is not the same as Qi generation. Patch 1.31 says a larger pool takes
longer to fill but increases Energy and Stamina gained on breakthrough.
For raw time to the next breakthrough, a smaller pool may be faster if the
rate is unchanged; for difficult tribulations, the extra stats may be worth
the delay. The exact reward formula is not established.

## Reincarnate for sustained progress

The in-game guide says each previous reincarnation adds **+0.25× catch-up Qi
generation** while climbing back, switching off upon reaching the previous
highest realm. This helps the rebuild; it does not permanently accelerate
progress beyond that peak. The reward rises with the realm reached in this
life and also includes a contribution from final Dao Power, but the complete
formula is unknown.

Compare your preview, the next upgrade you can afford and the expected time
to your next breakthrough. A large single-life payout is not always the best
DSP per day: repeated rebuilding also has a cost. Rebirth resets substantial
progress, so check the game's carryover screen and warnings before committing.
Never begin an irreversible rebirth merely to test the preview.

### Useful alternatives after the foundation

- **Earlier starting realms:** Rooted Foundation starts at Body Tempering;
  Flowing Meridians 1 plus the node costs **90 DSP**. Tempered Rebirth starts
  at Qi Condensation and brings that route to **338 DSP**. Buy when early
  replay time outweighs the benefit of more Qi or Soul Wandering.
- **Manual retention:** Enlightened Heart 1 → Echo of the Soul → Remnant
  Soul Memory costs **132 DSP**, retaining the active Soul technique up to
  level 10 if previously reached. The Mind and Body paths have the same
  initial prices. Choose the aspect you actually need; check trait gates.
- **Manual hunting:** Tireless Fist → Beast Lore 1 → Bountiful Hunt 1 costs
  **355 DSP** and adds 10 daily Bonus Yield charges. This pays off if you
  regularly spend those charges; the hunt bonus is for manual wins, not a
  verified auto-combat bonus.
- **Tribulations:** Tribulation Memory costs **120 DSP** after Flowing
  Meridians 1 and provides a temporary ward based on health. It may save time
  if surviving major breakthroughs is your bottleneck.
- **Traits:** Bloodline and Physique Memory are expensive deeper goals; each
  costs **2,329 DSP from zero** including its starting-realm path. Consider
  them when retaining an exceptional trait saves more time than the alternatives.

## A repeatable daily routine

1. Check that the intended primary action and, once unlocked, Second Path are
   active. Check Qi pool status and break through when ready.
2. Advance the Mortal Road when practical; it introduces systems and gives
   useful rewards. Equip a workable build and prepare for the next trial.
3. Spend Bonus Yield charges on manual wins that help your build. The patch
   describes a daily refill and 30 base charges; not a claim that unused
   charges accumulate indefinitely.
4. Check manual comprehension, gear and cultivation boosts; compare whether
   upgrading them is faster than waiting for Qi.
5. Before going offline, choose the action pairing that matches your current
   goal. Recheck the in-game rates instead of assuming theoretical stacking.
6. When progress stalls, compare the next breakthrough with the rebirth
   preview and your next planned Soul Tree purchase.
