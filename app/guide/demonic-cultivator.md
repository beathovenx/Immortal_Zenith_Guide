# The demonic path: a fresh-character darkness / yin guide

**Build:** PvE, Mental Fortitude-led yin offense, with Energy sustain and a
non-yin backup. **Evidence:** official public wiki snapshot dated 12 November
2026 and the recovered 1.31.0 client. This is a new-character plan, not the
lightning character's equipment or checklist.

## 1. What "demonic" means here

The combat element is **yin**. Darkness, shadow and netherworld are the theme,
not extra damage types. This is not a verified lifesteal, poison or automatic
healing-on-hit build.

**Demonic sect alignment is a separate system.** The client describes sects
as player organizations and says demonic recruitment requires sufficiently
negative morality ("Your Name"). Using yin is not evidence that your morality
changes or that you belong to a demonic sect.

The practical destination is:

- **Blood Moon Sovereign Bloodline (S):** pure-yin, Mental Fortitude-scaling
  trait attacks; no listed drawback.
- **Abyssal Heart Physique (S):** Energy, Mental Fortitude and defense support,
  a water backup attack, and late yin absorption; no listed drawback.
- **Mind:** a developed Mental Fortitude manual early, then an obtainable yin
  manual such as Nether Soul Sutra. Silent Yin is an alternative, not a
  required extra purchase.
- **Soul:** use an available functional bridge, then Moonshadow Sutra or
  Shadow Moon Sutra if obtainable.
- **Body:** keep a useful defensive manual. No Body-slot yin-granting manual
  was found in the saved manual catalogue; do not force a themed replacement.

These are **build targets, not confirmed vendors or guaranteed drops**.
There is no need to obtain S rank to start, or every intermediate to finish.

## 2. Creation and the first playable foundation

1. Inspect your actual starting bloodline and physique. Keep a strong roll
   even if it is not yin. Do not repeatedly restart just to chase a rare name.
   No optimal origin or yin-guaranteeing creation roll has been verified.
2. Learn the **Strength Training Basic Manual** from your bag if not already
   learned. Its public description says it is granted to every new disciple.
   Use it on **Body** at Human; comprehension 30 grants **Trained Reflexes**.
   That is an unlock, not a claim that it is your first yin damage art.
3. Keep cultivation and affordable equipment progressing. A weapon's useful
   stats matter more than a sinister name. There is no verified required
   "demonic weapon" for this route.
4. At **Body Tempering (major 1)** the client enables Soul and Mind cultivation
   (they are disabled while `major < 1`). Start a useful available Mind manual.
   **Astral Core Manual**, tier 0, is a candidate if obtainable: its growth
   emphasizes Mental Fortitude and its comprehension-60 art is **spirit**,
   not yin. It is not a guaranteed starter reward.
5. If you acquire **Shadow Weasel (D)** or **Moonlit Fox (C)**, their first yin
   attack unlocks at Body Tempering. Open **Tech -> Techniques**, inspect the
   learned art and equip it. Owning a manual or essence alone is not enough.

Mental Fortitude is the main offensive investment for the recommended
bloodline arts. The client also links it to comprehension and tribulation
steadiness. Energy funds those attacks; **Energy-scaling Soul arts and
Mental Fortitude-scaling Mind arts are not interchangeable**.

| **Blood Moon Sovereign, S** | Main long-term target. Yin attacks at major 3, 7 and 11; yin absorption at major 11. | No listed drawback, but availability and named-item drop odds are unverified. Do not stall for it. |
| **Netherworld Monarch, S** | Alternative for a more underworld-themed yin/spirit hybrid, if it arrives instead. | Major-7 art is **spirit and Energy-scaling**; major-3 and major-11 arts use Mental Fortitude. Do not make an S-to-S sidegrade just for the name. |

**Preferred flexible route:** keep your creation roll -> obtainable Moonlit
Fox -> optional Void Moth or Blood Moon Wolf -> Blood Moon Sovereign.
Shadow Weasel is a budget entry, not a required first purchase. Skip straight
to a suitable higher rank when practical.

## 4. Physique route: protect the caster

| Candidate | Role | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shadow Veil, D** | Budget yin/mind foundation. Mental Fortitude buff at major 1; **Shadow Veil Restoration**, an Energy-scaling heal, at major 3. | Initial Stamina -6 and -2%, Fortune -4. Replacing it removes the heal it granted. |
| **Twilight Flesh, C** | Useful early caster bridge: initial Mental Fortitude +6 and +5%, defensive bonuses, Mental Fortitude buff at major 1. | Initial Stamina -9 and -5%. Usually easier to accommodate in this Energy-led plan than reducing Energy itself. |
| **Moonshadow Veins, B** | Optional defensive/Mental Fortitude intermediate. Mixed yin/mind attack at major 7. | Initial Energy -9 and -7%, Speed -10. It does **not** list yin absorption; inspect your usable Energy before upgrading. |
| **Eclipsed Soul, A** | Optional later bridge: Mental Fortitude, yin/spirit defenses and yin absorption at major 8. | Initial Energy -10, Speed -14 and -8%. A larger Qi pool can also lengthen cultivation. |
| **Abyssal Heart, S** | Long-term target: initial Energy +31 and +14%, Mental Fortitude +31, Defense +12%, Damage Reduction +10%. Water backup at major 3; mixed yin/water art and yin absorption at major 11. | No listed drawback. Its first art scales with **resistance**, not Mental Fortitude; do not treat it as your main yin nuke. |

**Do not blindly take Moon Veil (A) as the next rung:** it lists initial
Mental Fortitude -16 and -7%, which works against the main scaling stat.
A higher rank can still be useful, but compare the whole panel.

Keep a good starting physique rather than hunting every themed intermediate.
Twilight Flesh can remain useful until a genuinely better acquisition arrives.

### The absorption trap

Eclipsed Soul supplies yin absorption at **major 8**; Abyssal Heart supplies
it at **major 11**. Replacing Eclipsed Soul before major 11 can therefore
remove absorption. A developed yin manual with an absorption milestone can
provide coverage: verify **ABSORB** on the yin affinity tile after any swap.

Absorption heals you when enemies hit you with that element. It is **not**
lifesteal from your own yin attacks.

## 5. Realm-by-realm progression

All book names below are conditional on finding an affordable, usable copy.
Manual tier is not the same as the granted art's `minRealmMajor`. Check the
actual Learn/use requirement in the item panel before spending.

| Realm | What to do | What not to wait for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Human (0)** | Body cultivation with the starter manual; improve basic equipment and survive easy fights. Inspect the traits you actually rolled. | A perfect origin, S-rank traits, or a yin manual. Soul/Mind are still locked. |
| **Body Tempering (1)** | Soul/Mind become selectable. Start a useful Mind manual; use a trait-granted yin art if you obtained Shadow Weasel or Moonlit Fox. Keep a functional non-yin attack if you have one. | No tier-0 to tier-3 yin-granting manual appears in this snapshot. Do not stop cultivation looking for one. |
| **Qi Condensation (2)** | Develop Mental Fortitude, Energy capacity and defenses. Blood Moon Wolf's first art is now realm-eligible if owned. Look for an affordable C/B bridge, not a full collection. | An A-rank bloodline or a themed weapon. |
| **Core Formation (3)** | Shadow Weasel gains Devouring Dusk; Moonlit Fox gains mind coverage; Void Moth gains its yin art. Shadow Veil's heal is available if still owned. | The earliest manual arts' major-3 gates do not make their **tier-4 manuals** usable automatically. |
| **Golden Core (4)** | First tier-4 yin manual candidates: **Moonshadow Sutra (Soul)** and **Nether Soul Sutra (Mind)**. Work toward comprehension 45 for an attack and 65 for yin absorption. Prefer whichever slot/acquisition solves your actual problem. | Both books at once, or abandoning developed defenses to acquire them. |
| **Nascent Soul (5)** | **Shadow Moon Sutra (Soul, T5)** is an optional Energy-regen/heal project. **Silent Yin Manual (Mind, T5)** is a Mental Fortitude-oriented alternative. Keep a mature T4 manual if it works. | Replacing Moonshadow solely because the new book is one tier higher. |
| **Immortal Ascension (6)** | Moonshadow/Nether Soul second arts require comprehension 90 and major 6. Optional T6 **Void Soul Codex** offers a later Mind route with an Energy-oriented growth profile. | Restarting every Mind manual; the route does not need all Void books. |
| **Immortal Saint (7)** | Shadow Moon's comprehension-90 heal and Silent Yin's comprehension-90 buff meet their realm gates. Void Moth/Moonshadow Veins and S bloodlines have milestone arts here if owned. | A mandatory trait change. |
| **Immortal Emperor (8)** | Eclipsed Soul can provide yin absorption. **Dark Moon Sovereign Sutra (Soul, T8)** is an optional offensive project, not a compulsory replacement for a sustain manual. | Losing the absorption or healing that keeps you alive merely to increase rarity. |
| **Shattered Void (9)** | **Eternal Yin Emperor Codex (Mind, T9)** is an optional late project. Level 45 grants a Mental Fortitude-scaled yin attack; its level-90 heal has a separate major-11 gate. | Relearning a Mind manual when a developed one already clears your content. |
| **Void Emperor (10)** | Optional **Void Yin Emperor Canon (Soul, T10)**: attack at 45, absorption at 65; its level-90 ward is gated to major 12. Dark Moon's level-90 art meets its major-10 gate. | Buying every late Soul book. Choose offense, sustain or defense deliberately. |
| **Celestial Immortal (11)** | Blood Moon Sovereign and Abyssal Heart reach their late yin arts and yin absorption milestones if owned. Eternal Yin Emperor's level-90 heal becomes realm-eligible. | S+ or SSS traits as a prerequisite for a working build. |
| **Celestial Saint -> World Creator (12-14)** | Finish chosen manual milestones, maintain non-yin coverage, and optimize for actual enemies. Void Yin Emperor's ward is realm-eligible at major 12. | A compulsory new bloodline or physique at every realm. |

### Avoid the name trap

**Abyssal Yin Scripture (T1)** grants a **water** attack at comprehension 60.
**Frozen Yin Codex (T3)** grants a **water** attack at 45 and a heal at 90.
They can be useful bridges, but neither establishes early yin offense.

The snapshot's first actual yin-granting manuals are **Moonshadow Sutra**
and **Nether Soul Sutra**, both tier 4. Earlier yin offense in this route
comes from traits if acquired, not an invented starting book.

## 6. Battle arts and quantitative priorities

These are the public technique fields, **not final damage/healing predictions**.
Mitigation, elemental attack/resistance and other live modifiers still matter.
Cooldown and effect names are recorded without assuming an undocumented
duration, stacking rule or exact weaken percentage.

| Art | Unlock | Raw base + scaling | Energy / cooldown | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Umbral Nip** | Shadow Weasel; major 1 | 22 + 0.70 x Mental Fortitude | 12 / 0 | Budget yin hit |
| **Moonlit Fox Duskrend** | Moonlit Fox; major 1 | 38 + 0.95 x Mental Fortitude | 20 / 1 | Early yin hit |
| **Devouring Dusk** | Shadow Weasel; major 3 | 44 + 0.90 x Mental Fortitude | 25 / 2 | Yin hit with weaken; **not** verified lifesteal |
| **Shadow Veil Restoration** | Shadow Veil; major 3 | Heal: 29 + 0.40 x Energy | 18 / 2 | Early heal if still owned |
| **Nether Soul: Nightwater of Nine Cold Springs** | Nether Soul 45; art major 3, book T4 | 300 + 0.98 x Mental Fortitude | 28 / 2 | Main Mind-slot yin hit, weaken |
| **Moonshadow: Turning Year Does Not Warm** | Moonshadow 45; art major 3, book T4 | 300 + 0.98 x Energy | 28 / 2 | Soul-slot yin hit, weaken |
| **Shadow Moon: Waning** | Shadow Moon 90; major 7, book T5 | Heal: 2,750 + 0.5355 x Energy | 40 / 3 | Later sustain |
| **Unbowed Remittance** | Blood Moon Sovereign; major 3 | 92 + 1.25 x Mental Fortitude | 38 / 1 | S-trait yin hit |
| **Scoured Closure** | Blood Moon Sovereign; major 11 | 138 + 1.45 x Mental Fortitude | 53 / 3 | Late yin hit, weaken |

For example, at 100 Mental Fortitude, Moonlit Fox Duskrend's displayed
base-plus-scaling component is **133**, and Umbral Nip's is **92**.
That is a comparison of raw components, not guaranteed combat damage or DPS.
An extra 100 Mental Fortitude adds 95 and 70 respectively to those components;
it does not increase Moonshadow's **Energy**-scaled component.

**Early battle bar:** one usable damage art, an available defensive buff/heal,
and non-yin coverage when learned. Do not fill the bar with expensive attacks
your Energy cannot fund. Listed HP/Energy/Stamina regeneration is described
as **out-of-combat** regeneration by the client; do not assume Energy Regen
provides an unlimited in-battle loop.

**Later bar:** main yin damage, a learned heal or defensive art, a different
element for yin-resistant/absorbing enemies, and an optional second damage art.
Check resource costs and cooldown availability in real fights. Mind, spirit
and water are possible coverage from this route, not universally safe counters.

## 7. Acquiring the build without inventing a farm

### What is actually known

The wiki's trait `dropsFrom` fields give these **weighted categories**:

| Target | Listed category |
| --- | --- |
| Shadow Weasel | rodent |
| Moonlit Fox | vulpine |
| Void Moth | insectoid |
| Blood Moon Wolf | lupine |
| Blood Moon Sovereign / Netherworld Monarch | humanoid |
| Shadow Veil / Twilight Flesh / Moonshadow Veins | yin |
| Eclipsed Soul | spirit |
| Abyssal Heart | water |

Examples from the creature snapshot:

- **Moon Hare**, **Shadow Step Rabbit**, **Shadow Panther**: tier 0, yin.
  Relevant to the yin physique category, **not** proof of a named essence.
- **Young Fox**, **Young Crystal Fox**: tier 0, vulpine. Relevant to the
  Moonlit Fox category even though their elements are physical and earth.

Use the combat lobby and **Achievements -> Collections** to verify location,
access and whether a fight is safe. The creature snapshot does not establish
which map spawn your new character can reach.

Patch 1.13.0 describes hidden category-weighted trait drops from hunting,
auto-combat and exploration fights. Ordinary creature drop lists do not
enumerate that hidden roll. **Named-crystal odds, rank eligibility and a
guaranteed target beast are not established.** Do not interpret "humanoid"
as a reason to attack players, or a low-tier fight as a proven S-rank farm.
Auto-combat itself requires Tireless Fist in the Soul Tree and ten wins
against that foe; do not assume a fresh account has it.

### Practical acquisition routine

1. Search the exact live item in **Collections** and inspect source information.
2. Inspect accessible town bookshops, sect merit stock and **World -> Dao
   Market**. These are places to check, **not verified sellers of these books**.
3. Read the actual copy's realm requirement, cost and stats before buying.
   Keep enough resources for usable equipment and progression.
4. If unavailable, keep a developed manual/current trait and continue advancing.
   Do not substitute an unverified "guaranteed drop" claim.

**No exact vendor, recipe, quest reward or live market listing for the yin
manuals or target essences was verified for this guide.** Only the starter
Strength Training Basic Manual has an explicit new-disciple grant description.

## 8. If you also want an actual demonic sect

This is optional role-play/social progression, separate from the combat plan:

1. Open your character's **Your Name** morality panel and ledger. Read its
   current demonic-entry threshold and the lower threshold that excludes
   other sect paths. The client supplies those numbers dynamically; no
   fixed cutoff is asserted here.
2. Explore available sects and inspect **Demonic** recruitment requirements
   before committing to a morality direction. Sects can have additional
   admission requirements and player approval.
3. Ordinary encounter choices and sect activities can move morality; read
   their actual shown consequences and consult the ledger. Do not assume
   equipping yin traits changes it, or that every mission moves it downward.
4. Prefer a helpful existing sect to founding one merely for the label.
   The client describes founding as requiring a sufficiently high realm and
   a manual at 50 comprehension that is not already another sect's primary art.
5. Compare available support, arrays, missions and merit stock. A demonic
   alignment does not prove that the sect sells any named yin manual.

The client also says a sect whose combined member morality turns negative
is branded Demonic. Individual morality and the sect's chosen path are
therefore related but not identical. Joining is not guaranteed by your build.

## 9. Upgrade safety and next-session checklist

Equal-rank different essences can replace, fail, elevate or destroy the trait.
Only your **current** trait contributes its bonuses, milestones and arts.
After replacement, rebuild your battle bar and inspect absorption; do not
count on retaining Shadow Weasel's attack or Shadow Veil's heal.

- [ ] Learn/use the starter Body manual and keep cultivation active.
- [ ] At Body Tempering, begin a useful available Mind manual.
- [ ] Equip one real damage art; keep non-yin coverage rather than going unarmed.
- [ ] Pursue an affordable suitable trait upgrade, not every intermediate.
- [ ] At Golden Core, check availability of a T4 yin manual; 45/65 are useful goals.
- [ ] Before every swap, compare Mental Fortitude, Energy, defenses, lost arts and absorption.
- [ ] Treat demonic sect morality as an optional separate commitment.
- [ ] Continue progression if a named book or essence is unavailable.
